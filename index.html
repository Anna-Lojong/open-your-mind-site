A teaching to return to

Follow a daily or weekly rhythm, stay with a teaching, or choose an available slogan yourself. Explore modern wording, traditional wording where provided, and further encounters with a teaching.

Reflection, kept close

Write alongside a slogan or revisit your entries in the Journal. Your journal stays on your device. Export and import a backup whenever you choose.

At your own pace

Available teachings and your journal work offline. Optional morning and evening reminders are off until you enable them. No account is needed.

A growing collection

Open Your Mind is currently being prepared for its first Android release. Completed teachings are available in the app; unfinished ones are marked Coming soon. All app content and features remain free.
