Privacy Policy

Open Your Mind
Last reviewed: 10 November 2026
Contact: openyourmind.support@gmail.com

This policy describes the current Open Your Mind Android app and its informational website.

Open Your Mind is an independently created mind-training and reflection app intended for a general audience. It is not specifically directed at children, and no account is required to use the app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your journal reflections, their dates and identifiers, and the teaching and, where applicable, encounter identifiers associated with them. It also stores your saved teachings, wording and reminder preferences, practice rhythm, manual teaching selections and encounter history. This information supports the journal, bookmarks, settings and guided practice.

On Android, journal entries are saved in private app files and mirrored in the app's WebView local storage. Temporary files help ensure reliable saving. Other settings and progress are stored locally. Browser previews use the browser's own storage, separately from the installed Android app.

Journal files are not encrypted by the app. Their protection depends on your device's operating system and access controls.

The app has no journal backend and does not automatically transmit your reflections, bookmarks, preferences or practice history to Open Your Mind. There is no account system or automatic cloud synchronization. Open Your Mind cannot recover journal entries lost from your device.

Reminders and Android permissions

Morning and evening reminders are optional, independent of one another and disabled by default.

When you enable reminders, the app requests notification permission. Your selected times and schedules are used locally by Android's notification service. Notifications contain general practice or reflection messages, never journal text.

Android or battery-saving settings may delay notification delivery. You can adjust or disable reminders in the app's Settings or manage notification permissions through Android settings.

The app uses notification, restart-restoration and wake-lock permissions to support local reminders. It does not request special exact-alarm access.

Internet permission is included in the Capacitor Android environment. The reviewed app has no remote journal service or automatic app-data upload.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or broad shared storage or media. Journal import and export use files or destinations you select.

Manual export, import and sharing

You can back up your journal through Settings → Journal & Data.

Export creates an ordinary, unencrypted JSON file containing your journal entries. Android then opens its share sheet, allowing you to choose where to save or send the file.

The app initially writes a copy to its private export cache. This copy may remain until it is overwritten or removed through device or app-data controls. Closing the share sheet without saving or sending the file does not create an external backup.

To import a journal, you select a JSON file. The app validates its records, checks for duplicates and requests confirmation before merging or replacing entries.

Import is processed locally. The file is not uploaded to Open Your Mind. When merging, existing entries are retained if their identifiers conflict with imported entries. Replacing the journal requires separate confirmation.

If you save or share a backup using another app or cloud service, that provider receives the file and handles it according to its own privacy practices.

Journal backups may contain sensitive personal writing. Choose a trusted storage destination and protect exported files appropriately. The app does not automatically delete copies you have exported.

External links and voluntary contributions

The app includes links to external sources and an optional creator-support page. These links open in an external browser.

The support page is hosted by Ko-fi.

The app does not attach your journal entries, preferences or practice history to external links. External websites and browsers may process connection information, cookies and information you voluntarily provide according to their own privacy policies.

Contributions support the independent creator of Open Your Mind. All app content and features remain free, with no benefits tied to payment.

The app does not include a payment SDK, in-app checkout or payment tracking, and it does not store payment card information. Ko-fi and its payment providers process contributions.

Open Your Mind may receive information that these services make available to the creator, such as a contributor's display name, email address, message and transaction details. This information may be used to respond to contributions and administer creator support.

Messages may be publicly visible on Ko-fi depending on the options you select.

For further information, consult Ko-fi's privacy policy and the privacy policy of your chosen payment provider.

Third-party code and diagnostics

Open Your Mind uses React, Vite, Capacitor and Capacitor plugins for Android integration, local file handling, sharing, keyboard functionality and local notifications.

The reviewed app does not integrate advertising, analytics, remote crash reporting or a push-notification service.

Android and the WebView are provided by your device's platform and remain subject to the relevant system settings and privacy practices.

Development builds may provide local reminder diagnostics for troubleshooting. These contain information about reminder schedules, permissions and notification-channel status, but not journal text.

The production build excludes this diagnostic interface. The app has no automatic remote diagnostic-reporting service.

Retention, deletion and your control

Your journal entries, settings and practice history remain stored locally until you modify or delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

You can edit or delete individual reflections, remove bookmarks, change practice preferences and disable reminders.

Deleting a reflection removes it from your active journal. However, previously exported backups or cached copies may still contain that reflection.

Create a manual journal backup before clearing app data, uninstalling the app, changing devices or switching between app installations.

Android automatic backup is disabled, with explicit exclusions for cloud backup and device transfer in the reviewed Android configuration. You should not rely on Android's backup system to preserve your journal.

Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes its private data. Any backups saved or shared elsewhere must be deleted separately from those locations.

The app does not guarantee forensic secure erasure or deletion of third-party backups.

If you contact Open Your Mind by email, your email address, message and any information you choose to include are processed by the email provider and used to respond to and manage your request.

Please do not include private journal entries unless you intend to share them.

Correspondence is retained for as long as needed to handle your request and meet any applicable record-keeping obligations. Contribution records may be retained where required for accounting or legal purposes.

You can contact Open Your Mind to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information held by the creator.

Your locally stored journal is under your control and is not held on an Open Your Mind server.

This website

The Open Your Mind informational website is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages.

The reviewed website does not include a contact form, advertising, analytics scripts, embedded payment widgets, cookies or browser-storage functionality added by Open Your Mind.

Selecting an email link opens your email application. No message is sent unless you choose to send it.

When you visit a website, its hosting provider necessarily receives certain connection information, such as your IP address and the pages requested.

GitHub may process and log visitor information for security and service operation. For further details, see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Hosting and security logs are separate from the app's local journal. Their handling and retention are governed by the hosting provider's policies.

Open Your Mind does not operate website analytics or a journal-upload service.
