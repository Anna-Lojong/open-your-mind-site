Privacy Policy

Open Your Mind
Last reviewed: 10 November 2026
Contact: openyourmind.support@gmail.com

This policy describes the current Open Your Mind Android app and its informational website. Open Your Mind is an independently created mind-training and reflection app published Independent mind training, intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children. You do not need an account to use the app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your journal reflections, their dates and identifiers, and their associated teaching and, where applicable, encounter identifiers. It also stores saved teachings, wording and reminder preferences, practice rhythm, manual teaching selections and encounter history. This information supports the journal, bookmarks, settings and guided practice.

On Android, the journal is saved in app-private local files and mirrored in the app's WebView local storage. Temporary files support reliable saves. Other settings and progress are held in local storage. Browser previews use that browser's storage, separately from the installed Android app. These are not encrypted journal files; their protection depends on your device's operating-system protections and access controls.

The app has no journal backend and does not automatically transmit your reflections, bookmarks, preferences or practice history to Open Your Mind. There is no account system or automatic cloud synchronisation. Open Your Mind cannot recover a lost local journal for you.

Reminders and Android permissions

Morning and evening reminders are independent, optional and disabled by default. Notification permission is requested when you enable reminders. The selected local times and schedules are used on your device by Android's local notification service. Notifications contain generic practice or reflection messages, not journal text. Delivery may be delayed by Android or battery settings. You can change or disable reminders in the app's Settings and control notification permission in Android settings.

The app includes notification, restart-restoration and wake-lock permissions to support local reminders. It does not request special exact-alarm access. Internet permission is present for the Capacitor Android environment; the reviewed app has no remote journal service or automatic app-data upload. The app does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone or broad shared storage/media. Import/export uses files or destinations you choose.

Manual export, import and sharing

Settings > Journal & Data lets you export your journal as an ordinary, unencrypted JSON file. Android opens its share sheet so you choose whether and where to save or send it. The app first writes a copy to its private export cache. That copy can remain until overwritten or cleared through device/app-data controls. Cancelling the share sheet does not create an external backup.

Import reads a file you select, validates its journal records, checks for duplicates and asks for confirmation before merging or replacing entries. The file is processed locally and is not uploaded to Open Your Mind. Existing entries are kept when a merge encounters conflicting identifiers. Replacing the journal requires a separate confirmation.

If you share a backup through another app or cloud provider, that service receives the file and handles it under its own policies. Backups may contain sensitive personal writing: choose a trusted destination and protect the exported file. The app does not automatically delete exported copies.

External links and voluntary contributions

Source links and the optional creator-support link open an external browser. The support destination is Ko-fi. The app does not attach journal entries, preferences or practice history to these links. External websites and browsers handle connection information, cookies and any information you provide under their own policies.

Contributions support the independent creator. All app content and features remain free, with no benefits tied to payment. The app has no payment SDK, checkout or payment tracking and does not store card details. Ko-fi and its payment providers process contributions. Open Your Mind may receive contributor information made available by those services, such as a display name, email, message and transaction information, to respond to support and administer contributions. Public messages may be visible on Ko-fi according to your choices. Consult Ko-fi's privacy policy and your chosen payment provider's policy before contributing.

Third-party code and diagnostics

The app uses React, Vite, Capacitor and Capacitor plugins for Android integration, local files, sharing, keyboard handling and local notifications. No advertising, analytics, remote crash-reporting or push-notification service is integrated in the reviewed app. Android and the WebView are provided by the device/platform provider and remain subject to its settings and policies.

Development builds can expose local reminder diagnostics for troubleshooting. These describe reminder schedules and permission/channel state, not journal text; the production build excludes that diagnostic interface. The app has no automatic remote diagnostic-reporting endpoint.

Retention, deletion and your control

Your active journal, settings and progress remain locally until you change or remove them, clear app data or uninstall the app. You can edit or delete individual reflections, remove bookmarks, change practice settings, and disable reminders. Deleting a reflection removes it from the active journal; an earlier exported or cached copy may still contain it. Keep a manual backup before clearing data, uninstalling, changing phones or changing app installations.

Android automatic backup is disabled, with explicit cloud-backup and device-transfer exclusions in the reviewed Android configuration. Do not rely on Android backup to preserve your journal. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes app-private data; backups you saved or shared elsewhere must be deleted there separately. The app does not promise forensic secure erasure or deletion from third-party backups.

If you email Open Your Mind, your email address, message and any information you choose to include are processed by the email provider and used to answer and manage your request. Do not include private journal text unless you intend to share it. Correspondence is kept while needed to handle the request and any applicable record-keeping obligations. Contribution records, if any, may need to be retained for applicable accounting/legal obligations. You can contact OpenYourMind to ask about access, correction or deletion of information Open Your Mind actually holds; local journal content is controlled on your device, not held on a Open Your Mind server.

This website

The supplied static website has no contact form, advertising, analytics scripts, embedded payment widgets, cookies or browser-storage code of its own. Following the email link opens your email application; no message is sent until you choose to send it.

Serving a website necessarily exposes connection information such as your IP address and requested pages to its hosting provider. The public preview is hosted through OpenAI Sites and its hosting infrastructure; platform processing is governed by the OpenAI privacy policy. If the site is deployed to GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. Host service/security logs are separate from the app's local journal and follow the host's retention and privacy practices. Open Your Mind has not added website analytics or a journal-upload service.

Questions and changes

Contact OpenYourMind at openyourmind.support@gmail.com with privacy or support questions. The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and does not include an age-verification system. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to Open Your Mind, contact this address.

This policy will be updated when the app's data handling or hosting arrangements change. The review date above identifies this version. Review the current policy before sharing a backup, sending an email or following an external support link.
